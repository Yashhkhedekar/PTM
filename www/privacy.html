Privacy policy

Pratilipi Story Manager (PTM) is a personal tool for tracking your own stories, episodes, views, followers and earnings.

What we store

Your email address and the data you type into the app (account names, story names, numbers, dates and earnings). Nothing is collected from Pratilipi, and the app does not read your Pratilipi login.

Where it is stored

On Supabase servers, protected so that only you can read or change your own data. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Deleting your data

Open Export & data and choose Delete my account. Your account and all its data are removed permanently.
